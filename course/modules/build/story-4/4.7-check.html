Step 4.7: Check Story 4

The check calls your groups routes, creates a group and reads your GroupFilter code.

การตรวจจะเรียก route ของ groups สร้าง group และอ่านโค้ด GroupFilter ของคุณ

  PASS  GET /api/groups lists the groups with their task counts
  PASS  POST /api/groups creates a group
  PASS  POST /api/groups with an empty name answers 400
  PASS  GroupFilter.jsx loads and shows the groups
Hint

A 404 on /api/groups means the router is not mounted (step 4.3). A 500 is an error in the SQL: read the log with docker compose logs --tail 30 ws-main.
